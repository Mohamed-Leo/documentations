# Research Basis

This skill was assembled using current professional guidance and official resources as of September 2026. These sources inform the workflow; future agents should still research current markets, competitors, trademark registers, platforms, and accessibility standards when the task depends on them.

## Agent Skills format

- OpenAI Academy — Using skills: https://openai.com/academy/skills/
- OpenAI Academy — Skills resource: https://academy.openai.com/en/public/clubs/work-users-ynjqu/resources/skills

Key design implication: skills should package reusable workflow instructions plus supporting resources and checks, rather than rely on a single giant prompt.

## Brand systems and guidelines

- Frontify — Brand Guidelines: https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/brand-guidelines
- Frontify — Brand Strategy: https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/brand-strategy
- Frontify — Brand System: https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/brand-system
- Frontify — Visual Identity: https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/visual-identity
- Adobe — Brand Guidelines: https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/examples/brand-guidelines
- Adobe — How to Design a Logo: https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/how-to-design-a-logo

Key design implication: a professional identity connects strategic foundations, verbal identity, visual identity and practical usage rules; the logo is one component rather than the entire brand.

## Voice and tone

- Mailchimp Content Style Guide — Voice and Tone: https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/
- Mailchimp Content Style Guide: https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/

Key design implication: define a stable voice and context-sensitive tone, with actionable writing principles rather than adjectives alone.

## Trademark screening

- WIPO Global Brand Database: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database
- WIPO Madrid System — Search Before Filing: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/how_to/search/index
- USPTO — Trademark Basics: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics
- USPTO — Federal Trademark Searching: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching
- USPTO — Comprehensive Clearance Search: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks

Key design implication: search exact and similar names/designs and related goods/services; preliminary searching is not comprehensive legal clearance.

## Accessibility

- W3C Design System — Color Contrast: https://design-system.w3.org/settings/

Key design implication: brand palettes intended for digital UI must be tested in real text/background and UI combinations; do not assume a palette is accessible because the individual colors look distinct.
