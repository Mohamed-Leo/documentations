# Brand Identity Quality Gates

Run these before finalizing. A gate can be marked **pass**, **pass with risk**, or **fail / revise**.

## 1. Strategic coherence
- Audience is prioritized.
- Positioning names a meaningful difference.
- Promise is supportable.
- Reasons to believe exist.
- Personality is behaviorally defined.
- Future growth/architecture has been considered.
- Major identity choices can be traced to strategy.

## 2. Naming
- Name fits strategy and target perception.
- Pronunciation/spelling are acceptable in target languages.
- Candidate was compared against relevant competitors.
- Preliminary web/domain/social checks were performed when appropriate.
- Preliminary trademark screening was performed in relevant sources when appropriate.
- No legal-clearance claim is made.
- Risks and next steps are documented.

## 3. Verbal identity
- One-line description is understandable.
- Tagline is not being asked to do the job of positioning.
- Messaging pillars have proof.
- Voice includes actionable do/don't guidance.
- Tone adapts to context.
- Sample copy sounds specific to this brand rather than category-generic.

## 4. Visual identity
- Logo works in one color and small size.
- Symbol/wordmark is not an obvious imitation of a known mark.
- Identity has recognition devices beyond the logo.
- Color roles are defined.
- Key digital color pairs are accessibility-checked.
- Typefaces support required languages/weights and have a licensing plan.
- Imagery/iconography rules are observable, not adjective-only.
- Bilingual/RTL behavior is documented when relevant.

## 5. System usability
- Approved variants and misuse rules exist.
- At least the highest-priority real touchpoints have been tested.
- Assets/specs are organized.
- Guidelines identify a source of truth, owner and version.
- Another competent person/agent could produce an on-brand output from the guide.

## 6. Claims and ethics
- No fabricated customer results, certifications, awards, partnerships, data or capabilities.
- Sensitive cultural meanings have been considered where relevant.
- Distinctive expression is original rather than derivative.

## Final verdict format

Provide:
- overall readiness: **Concept / Direction Approved / Production Ready**
- passed gates
- unresolved risks
- decisions needing human/legal/production review
- next highest-value actions

Never label a concept “Production Ready” if vector masters, final font licensing, print specifications, legal clearance, or other required production checks remain unresolved.
