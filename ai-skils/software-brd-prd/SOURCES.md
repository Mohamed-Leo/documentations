# Research Basis

This skill was designed from cross-referenced software requirements and product-management guidance, including:

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 — requirements engineering processes and information items (current published edition; reviewed/confirmed in 2024). A third edition draft was under development in 2026.
  https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html
- IIBA Business Analysis Standard / BABOK-aligned requirement classification — business, stakeholder, solution (functional/non-functional), and transition requirements; traceability.
  https://www.iiba.org/knowledgehub/the-business-analysis-standard/4-implementing-business-analysis/4-4-understanding-requirements-and-designs/
- Atlassian — PRD purpose, goals, assumptions, user stories, design context, and explicit out-of-scope items; collaborative/living requirements.
  https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/requirements
- IBM — business, stakeholder/user, system, functional, non-functional, and domain requirements; requirements management and traceability.
  https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/what-is-requirements-management
- Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework — trace functional requirements to measurable business objectives, define NFR targets, and control in/out-of-scope boundaries.
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/cloud-native/plan-cloud-native-solutions
- NASA Software Engineering Handbook / requirements guidance — requirements quality characteristics such as clear, complete, consistent, feasible, necessary, singular, traceable, implementation-independent, unambiguous, and verifiable.
  https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVC/pages/100598308/Software%2BRequirements%2BAnalysis
  https://www.nasa.gov/reference/appendix-c-how-to-write-a-good-requirement/
- Agent Skills open specification — SKILL.md structure, progressive disclosure, references, and validation conventions.
  https://agentskills.io/specification
